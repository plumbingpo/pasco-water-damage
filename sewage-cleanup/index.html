Few emergencies in a building are as hazardous as sewage backing up into it. Raw sewage contains dangerous bacteria, viruses, parasites and other pathogens, including E. coli, hepatitis, salmonella and giardia, that pose serious health risks to anyone who touches contaminated materials. Across Pasco, Pasco Restoration answers sewage emergencies 24/7, sending trained technicians who arrive with EPA-registered disinfectants and the right protective equipment so your property is decontaminated safely.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. Because of it, cleanup follows the toughest rules in the trade, which means full protective gear, barriers to contain the area, tearing out any porous material that met sewage and disinfecting all that is left. Our technicians follow IICRC S500 standards for Category 3 water damage so your property is restored to safe occupancy standards.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. Lingering sewage odor is eliminated with thermal fogging and industrial hydroxyl generators, which we run in the affected area.

Maintenance Tips for Sewage Cleanup

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Schedule a camera inspection of the sewer line every 2 to 3 years so trouble is found early
  • A drain that is slowing down should be dealt with early, while it is still partly open
  • In an emergency a plumber needs the cleanout, so learn where yours is and keep it in good shape
  • Roots from trees close to the sewer line work their way into pipes and block them, so have them cleared
  • Wipes clog sewer lines even when the label says flushable, so only toilet paper goes down the toilet
  • Hair and other debris are easy to stop with simple strainers on tub, shower and sink drains

When to Call for Sewage Cleanup in Pasco

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Gurgling sounds from toilets or drains when other fixtures are used
  • Tubs, toilets or floor drains pushing black water back into the room
  • Soggy, smelly ground over the sewer line or leakage at the cleanout cap
  • A toilet overflow containing waste that has spread onto flooring or carpet
  • Lower levels or drain openings giving off a heavy sewage odor, sometimes like rotten eggs
  • Dark residue from sewage left on the basement floor or ringing a drain
  • More than one drain in the house overflowing or backing up together

The Benefits of Professional Sewage Cleanup

Sewage cleanup is not something any homeowner should attempt. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. Hepatitis, gastrointestinal disease and infections of the skin or respiratory system are among the serious illnesses these pathogens cause.

Contaminated materials count as biohazard waste, and disposing of them properly is part of a professional cleanup. Simply placing sewage-contaminated drywall, carpet and insulation in your regular trash is both illegal and hazardous to sanitation workers and the community. Licensed biohazard disposal facilities take this waste from certified restoration firms, which keep the required protocols in place.
